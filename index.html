    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything shop owners need to know.

    Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

    For door-to-door delivery on our scheduled route van, the minimum is just 5 cases (mix any brands or flavors). For walk-in counter pickups at our Pathanamthitta Central or Thiruvalla warehouses, there is zero minimum — you can pick even 1 single case.

    Can I mix different brands and bottle sizes in one order?

    Yes, absolutely! You can combine 2 cases of Cola, 1 case of Mango juice, 1 case of Goli soda, and 1 case of packaged water. Your total cases count together toward your wholesale tier discount.

    How does the sub-district branch selection work?

    We operate 5 distribution hubs covering all taluks across Pathanamthitta: Pathanamthitta Central Hub (Kozhencherry Taluk), Thiruvalla Commercial Hub, Adoor Central Depot, Ranni Highland Depot, and Konni & Mallappally Sub-Hub. Selecting your nearest branch routes your order directly to the local depot manager for faster dispatch.

    What payment modes are accepted?

    We accept Cash on Delivery, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and direct IMPS/NEFT bank transfer upon unloading. Regular retail accounts can apply for weekly revolving store credit after 30 days of consistent business.

    Do you provide visi-coolers or event chillers?

    Yes. Branded double-door and single-door visi-coolers are allocated free of charge to registered retailers maintaining monthly purchase volume. For outdoor events, marriages, and festivals, we rent out heavy chillers, insulated ice boxes, and supply commercial tube ice.
